# Depression Speech Evidence Table

This table summarizes Stage 2B evidence used for depression-related speech background and feature-family claims. It is not an evidence table for the project's numerical results; those remain tracked in `paper/PAPER_EVIDENCE_MAP.md`.

| Row | Evidence focus | Sources | Population or task context | Features or speech behavior | Supported manuscript use | Mixed findings or limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Speech as a depression-relevant behavioral modality | `leal2025survey` | Survey of speech-based depression assessment studies | Acoustic, prosodic, timing, cepstral/spectral and voice-quality markers | Abstract and introduction framing that speech can contain depression-relevant information. | Survey-level support only; not a clinical validation source. |
| 2 | Prosody and pitch/F0 | `leal2025survey`; `valstar2016avec` | Multi-study survey plus AVEC acoustic baseline context | F0/pitch, intonation, pitch-related functionals | Supports feature-family discussion in Sections 1 and 2.2. | F0 can depend on age, sex, task and recording conditions; direction of effect should not be overgeneralized. |
| 3 | Pause behavior and speech rate | `leal2025survey`; `valstar2016avec`; `tao2023featureCorrelation` | Survey, AVEC timing/acoustic descriptors and Androids read-speech features | Pause rate, voiced/unvoiced timing, speaking rate, voicing probability | Supports the manuscript's timing and pause-feature background. | DAIC response latency and Android reading/interview timing are not directly interchangeable. |
| 4 | Energy and intensity | `leal2025survey`; `tao2023featureCorrelation`; `zhang2025clinicalSpeech` | Survey, Androids read speech and a clinical speech corpus | RMS energy, short-term energy, loudness/intensity | Supports the statement that energy/intensity cues are common in depression speech studies. | Energy can be affected by microphone, normalization, speaking style and prompt design. |
| 5 | Cepstral and spectral features | `leal2025survey`; `tao2023featureCorrelation`; `yin2023transformerSpeech` | Survey plus empirical depression-detection studies | MFCCs, spectral descriptors and model inputs | Supports MFCC/cepstral categories in the handcrafted-feature discussion. | Neural-model use of MFCCs does not make MFCCs uniquely diagnostic or task-invariant. |
| 6 | Voice quality | `leal2025survey`; `valstar2016avec` | Survey plus AVEC/COVAREP baseline feature context | Voice-quality descriptors, glottal/phonation-related measures | Supports inclusion of voice quality among handcrafted acoustic feature families. | Voice-quality measures are recording- and speaker-dependent. |
| 7 | Feature relationships rather than isolated descriptors | `tao2023featureCorrelation` | Androids read-speech subset | Correlation matrices over acoustic low-level descriptors | Supports cautious wording that depression-related information may appear in feature relationships. | Androids-specific read-speech result; not direct support for cross-corpus transfer. |
| 8 | Clinical-data speech features | `zhang2025clinicalSpeech` | COAD-Corpus with clinician-designed prompts | Mel spectrogram, MFCC and short-term energy | Adds primary clinical-data context for energy/MFCC evidence. | Modest sample and constrained prompts; not used for deployment or ethics claims. |
| 9 | Study heterogeneity | `leal2025survey` | Survey of datasets, labels and elicitation tasks | Dataset, speech type, label and protocol variation | Supports Section 2.1 statement that cross-study comparison is difficult. | Does not replace dedicated metric or domain-shift references. |
