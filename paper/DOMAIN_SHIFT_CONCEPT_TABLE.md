# Domain Shift Concept Table

This table defines the shift concepts used to interpret Stage 2C. It keeps the manuscript's wording descriptive: the project reports observed feature shifts, score compression and target-domain classifier gains, but does not claim to have isolated a single causal shift mechanism.

| Concept | Compact definition | Stage 2C operational relevance | Supporting source | Interpretation guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Covariate shift | The feature distribution differs between source and target while the prediction rule may be assumed stable. | DAIC and Android WavLM embeddings showed large standardized feature shifts. | `morenoTorres2012datasetShift` | Feature shifts alone do not prove a clinically meaningful biomarker. |
| Prior probability shift | The class-prior distribution differs between source and target. | DAIC PHQ-8 binary prevalence and Android HC/PT group balance are not identical constructs. | `morenoTorres2012datasetShift` | Class-prior differences do not explain all transfer failure by themselves. |
| Concept or conditional shift | The relationship between features and labels differs across domains. | The Android-trained classifier achieved much higher ROC-AUC than the frozen DAIC classifier on the same frozen features. | `morenoTorres2012datasetShift` | This is a plausible framing, not a formal causal decomposition. |
| Label-definition shift | Source and target labels measure related but non-identical outcomes. | DAIC uses PHQ-8 binary labels; Android uses HC/PT group labels. | `tao2023androids`; `valstar2016avec`; `kroenke2009phq8` | Results should not be interpreted as transfer between identical clinical endpoints. |
| Task shift | The elicitation task changes across training and test conditions. | DAIC guided interview, Android interview and Android reading differ in lexical and interaction structure. | `tao2023androids`; `tao2023featureCorrelation`; `leal2025survey` | Task shifts can coexist with language, corpus and label shifts. |
| Language or cultural shift | Speech language or cultural context differs across corpora. | DAIC-WOZ uses English interview speech; Androids uses Italian speech. | `alghowinem2016crossCultural`; `tao2023androids`; `leal2025survey` | Language/culture is one component of transfer difficulty, not the sole explanation. |
| Recording or acquisition shift | Microphone, environment, segmentation or corpus-collection conditions differ. | DAIC and Android were collected under different protocols and preprocessing routes. | `leal2025survey`; `morenoTorres2012datasetShift` | The manuscript does not infer hardware-specific causal effects. |
| Score calibration shift | Predicted probabilities no longer match observed target-domain frequencies. | Frozen DAIC probabilities were compressed below 0.5 and the frozen DAIC threshold on Android interview speech. | `niculescuMizil2005probabilities`; `vanCalster2019calibration` | Calibration changes are distinct from ROC-AUC changes. |
| Threshold shift | The source decision threshold is poorly placed for target-domain scores. | Phase 2C Protocol B selected Android fold-specific thresholds while preserving frozen score ranking. | `morenoTorres2012datasetShift`; `vanCalster2019calibration` | Target-domain threshold adaptation, not pure zero-shot evaluation. |
| Classifier-boundary shift | A source-trained classifier boundary does not separate target-domain examples well, even if the frozen representation remains useful. | Protocol D learned a new Android logistic-regression boundary and improved ROC-AUC from 0.6412 to 0.8675. | `morenoTorres2012datasetShift`; `vanCalster2019calibration` | Used descriptively; the project evidence supplies the boundary comparison. |
| Representation transfer | The frozen encoder preserves some ranking information across domains or tasks. | DAIC-to-Android zero-shot ROC-AUC 0.6412 and strong within-Android classifier performance suggest reusable ranking information. | `baevski2020wav2vec2`; `chen2022wavlm`; `yang2021superb` | SSL representation transfer is not the same as classifier transfer. |
| Target-domain retraining | A new classifier is fit using labeled target-domain training folds. | Android-trained interview and reading classifiers used Android labels inside participant-safe folds. | `morenoTorres2012datasetShift`; `vanCalster2019calibration` | Within-Android validation, not DAIC-to-Android model transfer. |
