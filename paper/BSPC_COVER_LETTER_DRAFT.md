This manuscript reports a reproducible sequence of speech-signal experiments examining whether frozen self-supervised WavLM representations preserve depression-related information across corpora and speech tasks. The study separates representation transfer, threshold shift, calibration shift and classifier-boundary shift across DAIC-WOZ and Androids Corpus speech.

We believe the manuscript is appropriate for Biomedical Signal Processing and Control because it addresses biomedical speech-signal representation, cross-corpus signal-analysis robustness and practical limits of speech-based mental-health modeling. The main methodological contribution is a controlled comparison of frozen representation transfer versus target-domain classifier-boundary learning under participant-level safeguards.

The work is research-only. It does not claim clinical validation, diagnostic readiness, screening utility or deployment readiness. No prospective deployment study, demographic fairness audit or clinical utility study was performed.
