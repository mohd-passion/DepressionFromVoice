# Submission Strategy

No final submission sequence is selected in Stage 3B. The sequence below is conditional and must be approved after the authors provide journal preferences, APC constraints and indexing requirements.

## Conditional Sequence

| Step | Condition | Candidate group |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | If biomedical signal-processing fit and a no-APC subscription route are preferred | Biomedical Signal Processing and Control |
| 2 | If open access, digital-health audience and broad data/code transparency are preferred and APC is acceptable or covered | PLOS Digital Health |
| 3 | If digital mental health, speech AI and flexible length are preferred and APC support is available | Frontiers in Digital Health |
| 4 | If speech-processing readership is preferred over medical readership | Computer Speech & Language |
| 5 | If stronger medical-AI framing and AI-method novelty can be expanded | Artificial Intelligence in Medicine |
| 6 | If a broader speech-communication venue is needed | Speech Communication |

## Criteria for Moving to the Next Journal

Move to the next journal only after one of the following occurs:

- Desk rejection.
- Full rejection after review.
- Author decision that APC, data/code policy, article type or formatting requirements are incompatible.
- Journal asks for changes outside the validated scope of the manuscript or beyond what authors approve.

## Desk Rejection Policy

Record the journal, manuscript version, submission date, decision date, editor comments and reason for desk rejection. Update the candidate matrix only with verified information. Do not change experimental interpretations unless reviewer or editor feedback identifies a genuine manuscript error.

## Major Revision Policy

Before accepting a major revision invitation, authors should confirm:

- All requested experiments are feasible without violating frozen-source policy.
- Any requested clinical, fairness or deployment claims can be supported or explicitly declined.
- Data, code and ethics wording remains accurate.
- The revision can be completed without changing verified metrics unless a documented correction is required.

## Active Submission Policy

Maintain one active journal submission at a time. Do not submit the manuscript, a close variant or a translated version to another journal while one journal is considering it.

## Version History

Preserve a clear version history for:

- Author-approved manuscript versions.
- Journal-specific formatting versions.
- Figure-generation versions.
- Supplement versions.
- Reviewer-response drafts.

## Pre-Submission Checks

Immediately before submission:

- Recheck journal requirements from official pages.
- Recheck APC, waiver and discount information.
- Recheck data, code, ethics, AI-disclosure and preprint policies.
- Update references and bibliography formatting.
- Confirm author list, affiliations, ORCID values and contribution statements.
- Confirm funding, competing interests, ethics and consent statements.
- Confirm that figures and supplementary files contain no restricted audio, embeddings, participant predictions, fitted models or private data.

## Simultaneous Submission

Avoid simultaneous submission. The submission declaration should state that the manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere only after the author team confirms this is true.
