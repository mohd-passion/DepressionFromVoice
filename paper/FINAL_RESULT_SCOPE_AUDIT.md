## Required Scope Findings

- Android-trained results are never described as external validation in the manuscript.
- Target-adapted threshold and calibration protocols are explicitly not called pure zero-shot evaluation.
- Phase 1F is described as a provisional local labeled DAIC test, not official AVEC hidden-test performance.
- Phase 2E is described as cross-task transfer within Androids Corpus, not cross-corpus evaluation.
- No result record is described as clinically validated or deployment-ready.
