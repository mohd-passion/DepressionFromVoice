# Journal Selection Criteria

These criteria are provisional. Authors may change the weights before selecting the final journal.

| Criterion | Default weight | How to score | Author-adjusted weight |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Scope fit | 25% | Does the journal explicitly welcome the manuscript's domain, data type and research question? | Prioritize biomedical signal processing, speech/language processing, medical AI/digital health and affective computing. |
| Methodological fit | 20% | Does the journal value cross-corpus evaluation, self-supervised speech representations and reproducibility details? | Preserve cross-corpus, cross-task and frozen WavLM evaluation as the core contribution. |
| Audience fit | 15% | Will likely readers understand both speech-processing and mental-health AI aspects? | Prefer biomedical signal-processing readership with speech/language and medical-AI overlap. |
| Manuscript-length compatibility | 10% | Can the 5,309-word draft fit without damaging methodological transparency? | Prefer candidates where adaptation is feasible without changing validated results. |
| Open-access affordability | 10% | Is the APC acceptable or is a no-APC subscription route available? | Essential filter: must have a no-APC subscription route unless author later approves APC support. |
| Data and code policy compatibility | 10% | Can restricted datasets, excluded raw audio and public code artifacts be explained under the journal policy? | Keep restricted-data and public-code wording transparent and author-confirmed. |
| Figure/supplement compatibility | 5% | Can six tables, seven figure specifications and eleven supplementary candidates be accommodated or trimmed? | Prefer candidates that allow concise main figures plus supplement. |
| Author preference | 5% | Does the author team prefer a medical, digital-health, speech-processing or biomedical-engineering audience? | Apply preliminary author preferences only; final choice requires author confirmation. |

## Author Preference Questions

| Question | Author answer |
|---|---|
| Is a no-APC or subscription route required? | Yes. |
| What is the maximum acceptable APC? | Not specified. |
| Is Q1 status mandatory? | No. |
| Is SCI/SCIE indexing mandatory? | No. |
| Is rapid publication important? | Fastest reasonable publication is preferred, but speed was not scored without current verification. |
| Is open access mandatory? | No. |
| Is a medical audience or computing audience preferred? | Biomedical signal processing, speech/language processing, medical AI/digital health and affective computing. |
| Is a journal with stronger speech-processing readership preferred? | Yes, if compatible with biomedical/digital-health fit and no-APC route. |
| Is a journal with stronger clinical readership preferred? | Medical AI/digital health is preferred, but clinical claims must remain research-only. |

## Selection Rule

Do not choose the final journal until the author team supplies APC limits, indexing requirements, open-access requirements and preferred audience. Bibliometric rankings, journal-selectivity claims and publication-speed claims must not be used unless they are rechecked from official or authoritative sources immediately before the decision.

## Stage 3C Selection Rule

The author has supplied preliminary preferences, so a provisional target may be selected. The final journal is still not selected until the author confirms the provisional target and current journal information is reverified.

Stage 3C applies these filters in order:

1. no-APC subscription route must be verified or the candidate is deferred;
2. strongest scope and audience fit is preferred;
3. open access is not mandatory;
4. SCI/SCIE and Q1 are preferred only if current authoritative information is reverified, not assumed;
5. publication speed is not scored without current authoritative verification.
