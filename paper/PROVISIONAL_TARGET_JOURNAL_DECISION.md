- It has the strongest combined fit for biomedical signal processing, speech-derived WavLM representations, depression-related classification and cross-corpus/cross-task evaluation.
- The Stage 3B matrix verified a compatible subscription route, which satisfies the preliminary no-APC requirement better than fully open-access APC-only candidates.
- The manuscript length is close to the verified full-paper guidance, so adaptation appears feasible without changing experiments or metrics.
- The paper's responsible-AI and clinical-limitation language can remain conservative while the signal-processing contribution is sharpened.
