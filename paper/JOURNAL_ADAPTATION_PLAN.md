# Journal Adaptation Plan

Provisional target: Biomedical Signal Processing and Control

Plan status: `awaiting_author_confirmation_for_target_journal`

This plan prepares the next editing step only. It does not authorize submission, final figure generation, DOCX/PDF export or changes to experimental metrics.

## Adaptation Principles

- Preserve all validated metrics, tables and scope restrictions.
- Do not run new experiments or alter frozen experiment outputs.
- Keep all clinical, diagnostic, deployment and fairness claims conservative.
- Use only verified journal requirements; mark unresolved requirements as `needs_reverification_before_submission`.
- Keep declarations unresolved until the author provides exact wording.

## Before Formatting Starts

| Item | Required action |

| Author approval | Confirm Biomedical Signal Processing and Control as the first target or provide a different target. |
| Article type | Confirm Full Paper or another article type after rechecking the official guide. |
| Declarations | Obtain funding, competing interests, ethics/exemption, consent, acknowledgements, preprint and AI-disclosure wording. |
| Author metadata | Confirm author order, final affiliation wording, corresponding author, ORCID values and CRediT roles. |

## Provisional Submission Sequence

1. Biomedical Signal Processing and Control, if author confirms the target and current no-APC/subscription route remains verified.
2. Computer Speech & Language, if the author prefers speech/language readership or the first target becomes incompatible.
3. Artificial Intelligence in Medicine or Speech Communication, depending on whether medical-AI or speech-communication framing is preferred.
4. PLOS Digital Health or Frontiers in Digital Health only if APC coverage, waiver support or a deliberate open-access decision is confirmed.

## Stop Conditions

Stop before journal-specific formatting if:

- author does not confirm the provisional target;
- current journal information cannot verify a no-APC subscription route;
- required declarations remain unavailable;
- the journal requires new experiments, clinical deployment evidence or unsupported claims;
- authors choose a different target or backup sequence.
