# Paper Claim Audit

This audit classifies major conclusions in the manuscript. It is intentionally conservative.

| Claim | Classification | Evidence | Allowed wording | Forbidden wording |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| WavLM representations carry depression-related information. | Directly demonstrated within studied datasets; cross-corpus component supported by limited zero-shot ranking. | Android-trained interview ROC-AUC 0.8675; Android-trained reading ROC-AUC 0.8796; cross-task ROC-AUCs 0.8475 and 0.8501; zero-shot interview ROC-AUC 0.6412. | "Frozen WavLM features retained useful depression-related ranking information in these corpora and tasks." | "WavLM diagnoses depression." |
| DAIC classifier transfers successfully to Android. | Not demonstrated. | Zero-shot interview ROC-AUC 0.6412 but all-negative predictions at frozen thresholds; zero-shot reading ROC-AUC 0.5795 with all-negative predictions. | "The DAIC classifier showed limited ranking transfer but failed as a thresholded classifier." | "The DAIC classifier successfully transferred to Android." |
| Android-specific classifier training improves performance. | Directly demonstrated within Android cross-validation. | Android-trained interview ROC-AUC 0.8675 versus frozen 0.6412; Android-trained reading ROC-AUC 0.8796 versus frozen 0.5795. | "Target-corpus classifier learning substantially improved within-Android discrimination." | "Android-trained models are externally validated." |
| Reading and interview tasks share useful representations. | Supported but exploratory. | Paired cross-task ROC-AUCs 0.8475 and 0.8501; within-participant cosine similarity 0.9273. | "Reading and interview representations shared substantial information in paired Android participants." | "Reading and interview are interchangeable clinical tasks." |
| Pooled-task training is superior. | Suggestive. | Pooled-to-reading ROC-AUC 0.9058 and pooled-to-interview ROC-AUC 0.9135 were highest point estimates, but gain intervals included zero. | "Pooled training produced the highest point estimates, with suggestive gains." | "Pooled training is definitively superior." |
| Results generalize beyond Androids Corpus. | Not demonstrated. | No independent third-corpus evaluation of Android-trained models. | "Further external validation is required." | "The Android-trained models generalize broadly." |
| Models are clinically valid. | Not demonstrated. | No prospective clinical validation, deployment study, fairness analysis or clinical threshold study. | "Research classification results only." | "Clinically valid model." |
| Models can diagnose depression. | Not demonstrated. | Labels are supervisory corpus labels; no diagnostic study was performed. | "Classified corpus labels under controlled protocols." | "Diagnoses depression." |
| DAIC timing features transfer directly to Android. | Not demonstrated and specifically rejected. | Phase 2A found 17 of 28 timing features exactly compatible; Candidate B direct transfer invalid. | "DAIC timing model was not directly transferable under frozen definitions." | "Timing model externally validates on Android." |
| Layer 9 is universally best. | Not demonstrated. | Layer 9 was selected on DAIC train-only nested CV. | "Layer 9 was selected for this study using DAIC development data." | "Layer 9 is universally optimal." |
| Android reading is better than Android interview. | Suggestive/descriptive only. | Phase 2D reading ROC-AUC 0.8796 versus Phase 2C interview ROC-AUC 0.8675 used different task-specific folds; Phase 2E matched paired estimates were 0.9048 and 0.9065. | "Reading and interview performed similarly under frozen WavLM representations." | "Reading is superior to interview speech." |
